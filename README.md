**Today Budget** is a modern, intuitive, and beautifully designed Android application engineered to help you manage your daily expenses effortlessly. Unlike classic, over-complex finance apps, **Today Budget** focuses on what truly matters today: **how much money you can spend right now.**

Available completely **free of charge, public, and open-source**.

## ✨ Key Features

- **💸 Automatic Daily Budget Calculation**: Instantly see the exact amount available for your active day. The app automatically recalculates the figure based on the remaining days in your monthly cycle and already recorded expenses.
- **🚀 Quick Entry via Emoji**: Log your most common daily expenses in a split second by pressing custom quick-category buttons (*Food*, *Home*, *Transport*, *Tobacco*, decorated with beautiful illustrative emojis).
- **📊 Interactive Charts & Trends**: View interactive donut charts to immediately grasp where your money goes, alongside detailed historical trend charts filtrable by individual category.
- **⚙️ Highly Customizable**:
  - Choose your preferred currency symbol.
  - Define which day of the month starts your custom budget cycle.
  - Customize the bookkeeping "start of the day" hour (e.g., 04:00 AM, perfect for night owls!).
  - Enable **Savings/Carry Over** to automatically roll over your savings (or deficits) from previous days and months into your active budget.
- **🔒 Private & Secure**: Operates entirely offline. Your sensitive data is saved exclusively on your device using a high-performance local SQLite database (Room). No external servers, maximum privacy.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Technologies

The application is built following the highest modern Android development standards:

- **Kotlin**: Reactive, robust, and expressive language.
- **Jetpack Compose**: Native declarative UI framework designed entirely with Google's modern declarative toolkit.
- **Material Design 3 (M3)**: Cohesive pastel colors, rounded containers ("Sweet Theme"), and excellent visual contrast matching both light and dark system appearances.
- **Room Database**: Lightweight local data persistence with built-in SQLite framework.
- **Kotlin Coroutines & Flow**: High-performance asynchronous resource managers and real-time interface rendering.
- **MVVM Architecture**: Dedicated Separation of Concerns (Model-View-ViewModel) to guarantee optimal modularity and ease of maintenance.
- **AI-Assisted Development**: Developed with the supportive assistance of **Google AI Studio** tools, fostering productivity, speed, and transparency in coding practices.
